Full license text: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html

It is used here only as a sane built-in default so hpwarn's Wayland
layer-shell overlay (which renders text itself, without a toolkit) always
has a font to draw with out of the box. You can still point `font_file` in
~/.config/hpwarn/config.toml at any other .ttf/.otf you own instead.
